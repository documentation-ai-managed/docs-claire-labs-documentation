## Implementation Strategy

Start with a pilot program to test and refine your approach.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assess Needs" icon="search">
    Identify specific assessment challenges and goals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Tools" icon="tool">
    Choose appropriate Claire Labs features for your needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pilot Program" icon="rocket">
    Test with a small group of students and teachers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scale Up" icon="trending-up">
    Expand usage based on pilot results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor & Adjust" icon="bar-chart">
    Continuously evaluate and improve your implementation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">Begin with one course or grade level to minimize disruption and allow for iterative improvements.</Callout>

## Student Experience

Focus on creating a supportive learning environment with AI assistance.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Feedback Timing" icon="clock">
    Provide timely feedback within 24-48 hours of submission.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Feedback Quality" icon="check-circle">
    Combine AI-generated feedback with personal teacher insights.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Student Agency" icon="user">
    Encourage students to review and reflect on AI feedback.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Expandable title="Managing Student Expectations" default-open="false">
  - Explain how AI assists in the assessment process
  - Emphasize that AI supplements, not replaces, teacher judgment
  - Encourage questions about AI-generated feedback
  - Highlight the benefits of faster, more consistent evaluation
</Expandable>

## Data Management and Privacy

Handle student data with care and comply with privacy regulations.

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="FERPA Compliance" default-open="false">
    Ensure all data handling practices meet FERPA requirements for student privacy.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Data Retention" default-open="false">
    Define clear policies for how long assessment data is stored and when it's deleted.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Access Controls" default-open="false">
    Implement role-based access to ensure only authorized personnel can view student data.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

| Data Type | Retention Period | Access Level |
|-----------|------------------|--------------|
| Assessment Scores | 7 years | Teachers, Admins |
| Student Feedback | 2 years | Students, Teachers |
| Analytics Data | 1 year | Admins only |

<Callout kind="danger">Always obtain proper consent for data collection and processing in accordance with local privacy laws.</Callout>

## Measuring Success

Track key metrics to evaluate the effectiveness of your implementation.

- **Student Outcomes**: Improved grades and learning engagement
- **Teacher Efficiency**: Time saved on grading and feedback
- **Assessment Quality**: Consistency and fairness of evaluations
- **User Satisfaction**: Feedback from teachers and students

<Expandable title="Success Metrics" default-open="false">
  | Metric | Target | Measurement |
  |--------|--------|-------------|
  | Grading Time | 50% reduction | Teacher surveys |
  | Feedback Quality | 4.5/5 rating | Student evaluations |
  | Assessment Consistency | 95% agreement | Inter-rater reliability |
</Expandable>
